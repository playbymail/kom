# Portals

The thirteen Portals of Medokh's World.
A portal is a [feature](world-map.md#features) on a hex, not a terrain type.

## Description

From outside, a portal is a wall of unmoving mist that repels intruders by a Power of Suggestion.

## Rules

| Rule            | Detail                                                                                            |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Entering        | An army must be shielded from the Power of Suggestion                                             |
| Occupied portal | An army may not enter a portal held by another player's army; it must attack and destroy it first |
| Defence         | An army on a portal during combat cannot be attacked from outside except by a shielded army       |
| Attacking out   | An army on a portal may attack any enemy army or settlement                                       |
| Teleport        | An army that starts the turn on a portal may use the Use Portal order                             |

### Shielding

The Holy Gateway ritual comes in three levels, depending on who grants it.

| Level | Granted by                     | Mystics required | Cost per army        | Regiments shielded                       |
|-------|--------------------------------|------------------|----------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Minor | Crusader Divine Reward         | 3                | 10 Jade, 5 Pearls    | About 50, plus 3 per extra Mystic        |
| Major | Warlock Lords, Knight Crusader | 6                | 20 Jade, 10 Pearls   | About 100, plus 6 per extra Mystic       |
| Great | High Priest                    | 10               | 30 Jade, 15 Pearls   | About 150, plus 10 per extra Mystic      |

A Key of Power shields about 25 regiments per Mystic with the army bearing the Key.

### Use Portal

```
UP (Army #) (Portal # 1-13)
```

- Teleport happens before army movement, so the army may move out of the exit portal the same turn.
- The army must be given movement orders to receive scout reports at the exit portal.
- Teleporting has no known danger but may briefly daze the army.

Use Portal is granted by the Warlock Lord, Knight Crusader, Crusader Divine Reward, and High Priest sheets.
