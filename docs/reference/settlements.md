# Settlements

The settlements of Medokh's World that exist before any player tribe builds one: Independent cities and gem mines, and the cities of the ancient races.
A settlement is a [feature](world-map.md#features) on a hex, not a terrain type.

Related references: [world map](world-map.md), [races](races.md), [portals](portals.md).

## Settlement types

From `medocs/docs/manual/settlements.md` (Dynamic Games).

| Type            | Notes                                                                      |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Town            | Main population centre; each player tribe starts with two                  |
| City            | A large Town converted to a City; cannot be built from scratch             |
| Fortification   | Military fort                                                              |
| Shrine          | Religious site; must be more than 30 leagues from other Shrines and Towers |
| Gem Mine        | A fortification over a source of Gemstones, built by a Surveyor            |
| Wizard's Tower  | A fortification converted by a Loremaster, Magician, or Sorcerer           |

## Locations

Locations are taken from two Talisman Games intelligence reports, which name the region of each settlement but not its hex:

- `medocs/docs/sheets/malkahai-special-report-iii.md`, "Independent Settlements"
- `medocs/docs/sheets/malkahai-special-report-iv.md`, "Ancient Settlements"

Region names are normalized to the [region table](world-map.md#regions); see [errata: region names](../errata.md#region-names).
No hex within a region is given for any settlement.

### Player tribes

Each player tribe starts with two Towns (`medocs/docs/manual/settlements.md`) in its [starting region](world-map.md#starting-regions).
No source places them within the region.

## Ruins

No source gives the location of any ancient ruin.
Ruins are found in play: the Survey Region order (`SR`, map type 1 or 2) maps all ruins in the army's region (`medocs/docs/manual/orders/military.md`).

## Open questions

- No hex is known for any settlement; positions within each region must be designed or generated.
- Six starting regions already hold a pre-placed settlement:

  | Region | Region name         | Starting tribe | Pre-placed settlement |
  |--------|---------------------|----------------|-----------------------|
  | 1,3    | Oakendale Valley    | 8, Elves       | Halfling city         |
  | 3,3    | Ironrock Mountains  | 9, Dwarves     | Elven city            |
  | 4,4    | Riverrun Forests    | 14, Elves      | Dwarven gem mine      |
  | 3,5    | Grasslands of Angar | 19, Humans     | Drow city             |
  | 10,6   | Lowlands of Logal   | 25, Humans     | Elven city            |
  | 5,9    | Sea of Grass        | 37, Humans     | Rock Troll city       |

  The settlement lists are from the Talisman Games era and the starting regions from the Dynamic Games map table, so the two may not describe the same campaign.
- Two pre-placed settlements are in the centre regions, where no tribe starts: Devil's Horn (6,5) has a Human city, and Devil's Fang (6,6) has a Dwarven city and an Orcish gem mine.
- Several regions hold more than one pre-placed settlement; for example, Whispering Dale (7,2) has a Kobold city, a Dwarven gem mine, and an Ogre city.
